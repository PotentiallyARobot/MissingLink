Challenges

a new brief every week — make something, share it, get seen
1 · ENTER
Make something that fits the brief, share it to the gallery, and put the challenge word in your title or prompt. Entries collect automatically — there's no form.
2 · GET SEEN
Entries are searchable by their tag, so anyone can pull up the full field and back the work they like.
3 · GET PAID
Half the pool splits between the top three by likes. The other half is paid out proportionally to everyone who entered and got at least one like — so a good piece earns even without winning.
ANY TIME
Tips work outside challenges too. Every ◈ Tip on the wall moves 10 tokens straight to the creator, immediately.
